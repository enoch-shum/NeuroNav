Hardware & the math underneath it

Three sensed nodes. One Euclidean rule.

An ESP32 at the center, a LiDAR eye on the head, an inertial sense of posture on the torso — and true 3D distance deciding what the body feels.

ESP32, 20Hz I2C polling
